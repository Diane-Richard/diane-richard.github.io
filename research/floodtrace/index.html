FloodTrace: Community-Centered Water Quality Monitoring in Urbanizing Tropical Settlements

Tracing how floods contaminate the water people rely on, to warn communities early.

role
Researcher
dates
Oct. 2026 – present
methods
environmental sensors, eDNA, household surveys

FloodTrace is a Stanford planning project on how floods carry contamination into the water people rely on in fast-growing tropical settlements in Kenya and Colombia. I’m part of the research team, working on the path from a flood to people’s health: which water sources get contaminated, what that means for health, and how to detect it early, from eDNA to household surveys.
