Research approach

Four connected questions drive my work:

  1. 01How do changes in water, soils and ecosystems affect people's health?
  2. 02How can we measure these links where data is scarce?
  3. 03How can models show where to protect and restore them?
  4. 04How can the communities most at risk shape the solutions?
