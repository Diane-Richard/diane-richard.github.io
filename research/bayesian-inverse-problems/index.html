Bayesian inverse problems: from a blurred signal to a contamination history

Recovering what the data can't show directly, with its uncertainty.

role
Course project
dates
Winter 2026
methods
Bayesian inversion, SVD, hyperparameter estimation, MATLAB

I restored a signal blurred by a measuring instrument, a classic inverse problem where direct inversion fails. I estimated the prior and noise levels from the data, then computed the best estimate of the true signal with 95% confidence intervals. I also used the same tools on a groundwater case: working back from a clay core sampled in 2018 to how contaminated the clay was when cleanup began, four years earlier.
