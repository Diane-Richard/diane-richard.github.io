Streamflow data assimilation

CEE 261D (Data Assimilation), Stanford

Data assimilation for a rainfall-runoff model

Tracking daily streamflow with an ensemble Kalman filter.

role
Course project, team of three
dates
Spring 2026
methods
ensemble Kalman filter, parameter estimation, MATLAB

We used an ensemble Kalman filter to track daily streamflow with HBV, a simple rainfall-runoff model, over ten years of data. The filter caught the timing and shape of flood peaks but overestimated flow overall, so we estimated the model’s parameters from the data. That raised the model’s fit to measured streamflow from 0.28 to 0.77 (1 is a perfect fit).
